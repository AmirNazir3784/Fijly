Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Who we are

FIJLY Studio (“FIJLY”, “we”, “us”) is a video production service for SaaS companies. This notice explains what data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you place an order, we also collect your company name, the video type, length and price you chose, and your project brief. This information is stored securely in our database, hosted by Supabase on servers in Mumbai, India.

Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth); we never see it and never store it with your orders. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us.

When you use the FIJLY Studio client portal, we store your account information, project data, uploaded files, and production history. This data belongs to your workspace and is only visible to your team and the FIJLY production team.

How we use your data

We use your account and order details to set up your FIJLY Studio workspace, respond to your enquiry and discuss your video project. We use portal data to manage your video production workflow. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. The client portal uses session tokens for authentication, which are essential for the service to function.

Third-party services

We use Supabase for database hosting and file storage. We use jsDelivr CDN to load the Supabase JavaScript library. If you sign in with Google, Google provides the sign-in. No other third-party services receive your data.

Data retention

Sign-up requests and orders that don’t become projects are retained for 12 months. Client portal data is retained for the duration of your service agreement plus 90 days. You can request deletion at any time by emailing hello@fijly.com.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing hello@fijly.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@fijly.com.

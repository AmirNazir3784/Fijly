Submitting a project records the video you chose, any add-ons, and the price calculated from our published price list at that moment. Nothing is charged on this website.

Milestone payments

Each project is paid in three parts: 15% to start the project, 45% when you approve the storyboard, and 40% on final delivery. We email you a PayPal invoice for each milestone; payment is made to PayPal, not on this website.

Work on a stage begins once the invoice for that stage is confirmed as paid. Your project stays as Awaiting Payment until the 15% project-start payment is received, we do not begin video production until the 45% storyboard milestone is settled, and final files are released on the 40% final payment.

Prices are confirmed when you submit a project. A later change to our published price list does not change the price of a project already submitted.

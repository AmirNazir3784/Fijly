Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Who we are

FIJLY Studio (“FIJLY”, “we”, “us”) is a video production service for SaaS companies. This notice explains what data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, company name, and project details. This information is stored securely in our database, hosted by Supabase on servers in Mumbai, India.

When you use the FIJLY Studio client portal, we store your account information, project data, uploaded files, and production history. This data belongs to your workspace and is only visible to your team and the FIJLY production team.

How we use your data

We use contact form submissions to respond to your inquiry and discuss potential video projects. We use portal data to manage your video production workflow. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. The client portal uses session tokens for authentication, which are essential for the service to function.

Third-party services

We use Supabase for database hosting and file storage. We use jsDelivr CDN to load the Supabase JavaScript library. No other third-party services receive your data.

Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained for 12 months. Client portal data is retained for the duration of your service agreement plus 90 days. You can request deletion at any time by emailing hello@fijly.com.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing hello@fijly.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@fijly.com.
